## Spatial membrane follow-up

`SpatialMembraneTests` verifies material and energy accounting for local damage.
`DirectionalToxinCreatesVisibleLocalRuptureWithoutChangingGeometry` pauses transport and
reaction, pipettes 0.5 mass of toxin at region 0 of the grazer membrane, and allows
normal FixedUpdate exposure for two seconds. It verifies rupture and death, intact
opposite-side material, mean integrity above 80%, an actual transparent boundary opening,
and an unchanged organism transform. This is a controlled test scenario, not the default
unmodified scene. The exact setup is recorded beside its render.
